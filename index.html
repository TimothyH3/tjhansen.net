Come Together Compass

A community resource platform connecting families and individuals to local assistance programs and emergency lifelines. This is my senior capstone project currently in development with a team of 5 engineers for the non-profit group Community Pay It Forward.

  • •Focus on Privacy: Minimal data persistence with optional user preferences. All chat features are processed locally in a Pinia store, and chat history is never saved.
  • •Built without API or License Fees: Integrates public U.S. Census Bureau geocoding and Leaflet/OSM without reliance on proprietary mapping services.
  • •Relevant Results: Feature chatbot assistant, preference-scored suggestions, and an administrative portal for listing verification.

Starlink Residential Cost Map

I travel full-time and move my Starlink address often. Because monthly rates vary between regions ($27.50 to $170+/mo) without an official price map, I made this tool to plan internet costs ahead of time.

  • •Visual cell map: Renders hexagonal cells and pricing zones ($27.50–$55 discount, $120 standard, and congested rates) so travelers know what to expect before moving.
  • •Lightweight design: Uses PostGIS caching within 25km and a quiet 10-minute edge job so queries stay fast without burdening Starlink's servers.
